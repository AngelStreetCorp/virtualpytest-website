Channel monitoring

Your channels and apps watched around the clock on real devices. You hear about a black screen before your viewers call, with the picture to prove it.

What is checked

CheckWhen it fires
Black screenthe picture stays black for 30 seconds
No signalthe box sends nothing (asleep, cable pulled)
Frozen picturethe picture stops moving for 30 seconds
No soundsilence on a channel that should have sound
Picture corruption, player errorthe picture breaks up or the app shows an error

The checks run on the devices your viewers use: set-top boxes, TV sticks, phones and browsers, or a wall that visits every channel of your line-up every few minutes. An automatic triage throws away standby screens, menus and pictures that really move, so you only get real problems.

How it alerts

  • By email, in Slack, or as a signed webhook into your own tools.
  • A message when the channel goes down and another when it is back, each with the proof frame and a link to the incident.
  • Escalation: an incident nobody acknowledged after the minutes you choose is sent again.
  • You see every message sent, and a target that stopped working shows up as failed.

The monthly SLA report

On the 1st of each month, a PDF you can forward: availability per channel against your target, every incident with start, end, cause and proof, mean time to detect and to resolve, compared with the month before. In English, French or German.

A public status page, if you want one

Off by default. Turn it on and your viewers get a page with the state of the channels you choose, open incidents in plain words and the last 90 days. No device or internal name is ever shown.

Price

Billed per monitored stream per month. A monitored stream is one device (or one wall) watching your channels. Ask us for a quote.
